# Asset notes

The Threefold icon is original artwork generated for this experiment on 21 October 2026: three cream paper conversation cards on a warm red background. The same identity is used for the two native apps and the experiment website.

Website conversation images are screenshots of the included native prototypes with fictional sample content. They are labelled as prototype captures, not captures of a connected service. The browser sketch animates those images and does not deliver messages.

The native ChatGPT-style sidebar includes small product marks extracted from the user-supplied visual reference. They are retained only as interface context. The WhatsApp-style demo uses system contact symbols, fictional names and a plain wallpaper. Third-party product names and marks belong to their respective owners and are not licensed by this project. Threefold is independent of OpenAI and WhatsApp.

System typography and SF Symbols are rendered through UIKit. The website uses locally available system fonts; it does not request third-party fonts or analytics.
